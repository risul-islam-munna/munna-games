Contact

Get in touch

Bug reports, feature ideas and plain questions about Bricks Crusher are all welcome — this is a one-person project and feedback really does steer it.

Email me

The best way to reach support. I usually reply within a couple of days — messages are read by me, not a support queue.

hello@munna.dev

Reporting a bug

Please include your phone model, Android version, the level number, and the steps that trigger it. A screenshot or short screen recording helps a lot.

Feature requests

Tell me what you'd like to see — new power-ups, brick types, skins or modes — and why. Challenge and Crazy modes are already on the list.

Freelance & business

Not about the app? For hiring, project work or partnerships, head to munna.dev.
