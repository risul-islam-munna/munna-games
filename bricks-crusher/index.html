Gameplay taking shape

What's already playable

Bricks Crusher isn't a concept — the core systems below are built and being played in internal testing right now.

Reinforced & indestructible bricks

Normal bricks fall in one hit, reinforced bricks take several, and indestructible blockers never break — they just shape how the ball moves. More brick types are on the roadmap.

Split & Burst power-ups

Break bricks for a chance at a Split pickup, which clones every ball in play, or a Burst, which fans a spread of new balls from the paddle. Grab one mid-rally to swing a level.

100-level star-rated campaign

12 hand-built levels to open the game, then a procedurally generated campaign that carries the same difficulty curve all the way to level 100. Every level is scored on completion, moves and time for one, two or three stars.

Diamonds economy

Earn Diamonds by clearing levels, hitting milestone levels, daily rewards and the Lucky Wheel. Spend them in the store on skins — Diamonds have no cash value and can't be bought.

22-skin store

11 ball skins and 11 paddle skins. Unlock them with Diamonds or, for some, a rewarded ad — purely cosmetic, never a gameplay edge. A few unlock free at level milestones.

Daily Rewards & Lucky Wheel

A 6-day daily reward cycle pays out Diamonds and boosters. The Lucky Wheel gives three free spins a day, plus one more for watching a rewarded ad, for a chance at Diamonds or a Split/Burst booster.

Offline, no account

Progress, Diamonds, stars and skins save to your device. No sign-up, same principle as Dual Play — an internet connection is only needed for ads and announcements.

Rewarded ads, always optional

Watch an ad for a Diamonds top-up, an extra booster, or a bonus wheel spin — never required to play or to progress. No banners, no interstitials.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Bricks Crusher out yet?

Not yet. It's in active development and isn't published on Google Play or the App Store. This page tracks real progress, updated as the game moves forward — not a promised release date.

What kind of game is it?

A neon Arkanoid-style brick breaker. Bounce a ball off a paddle to clear walls of bricks — some reinforced, some indestructible — catching Split and Burst power-ups along the way, across a star-rated campaign of 100 levels.

What are Diamonds and the Lucky Wheel?

Diamonds are the in-game currency, earned by clearing levels, daily rewards and the Lucky Wheel, then spent on cosmetic skins in the store. The Lucky Wheel gives three free spins a day, plus one more for watching a rewarded ad, for a chance at Diamonds or a power-up booster.

Will it be free?

The plan is free-to-play, supported by optional rewarded ads, the same model as Dual Play. Paddle and ball skins are cosmetic-only, unlockable with Diamonds or a rewarded ad — never pay-to-win, and there are no in-app purchases.

Which platform will it launch on?

Android first. An iOS build may follow later, the same way Dual Play's did.

How can I follow progress?

Check back on this page, follow along on GitHub, or reach out directly — there's no mailing list or waitlist for this one yet.

Not ready to play yet — but almost there

Bricks Crusher is still in development. While it's being finished, Dual Play is already out on Google Play.
