Local multiplayer

Together in the Same Room. Connected in the Game.

Turn your local Wi-Fi network into a multiplayer playground. Host a game, invite nearby friends, and enjoy competitive matches without relying on an internet game server.

  1. 1

    Connect to the same network

    Put your devices on the same Wi-Fi network, or have one player start a phone hotspot the others join.

  2. 2

    Host a supported game

    The host picks the game and settings. Everyone else opens the same game and sees the host in a list, with the board and rules previewed.

  3. 3

    Join and start playing

    Tap to join and the match starts. Talk while you play with in-match voice chat on supported Android Wi-Fi games.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How many games are available in Dual Play?

Ten: Tic Tac Toe, Four in a Row, Memory Match, Ludo, Dots and Boxes, Color Connect, Wall Block, Shape Crack, Sholo Guti and Bagh-Bandi. All ten play in the Android app and in your browser.

Is Dual Play free?

Yes — free to download and play. It is supported by ads. There is no paid account, no subscription and no premium games locked away.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Playing against a bot or passing one phone between two players works fully offline. Wi-Fi multiplayer needs the devices on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, but still no internet connection. Internet is only used to load ads and to fetch in-app announcements.

Can I play with friends on the same device?

Yes. Every game has a same-device mode where players take turns on one screen. In Color Connect, one player builds a puzzle and hands the phone over for the other to solve.

How does local Wi-Fi multiplayer work?

Put the devices on the same Wi-Fi network or a phone hotspot. One player hosts a game, the others open the same game and see the host in a list, then tap to join. Ludo rooms hold up to four devices; every other game is two players. Wi-Fi multiplayer is in the Android app.

Does Dual Play support voice chat?

Yes, in supported Wi-Fi matches on the Android app. Voice runs directly between the devices on your network, the microphone starts muted and audio is never recorded or stored. On some hotspots the connection may briefly use a public connection-assistance service to get started. Color Connect Wi-Fi games do not have voice chat.

What are Achievement Ranks?

Eight ranks — Rookie, Explorer, Challenger, Skilled Player, Expert, Master, Champion and Legend — that reflect your overall rating from 0 to 100. Higher ranks also ask you to explore more of the games, so progress comes from variety as well as wins.

How can I improve my rating?

Win matches against the bots, try harder difficulty levels (they are worth more) and play more of the games in the collection. Only matches against bots and puzzle solves count toward your rating; Wi-Fi and same-device matches are for fun and do not change it. Ranks follow your current performance, so they can move down as well as up.

Can I share my achievements?

Yes. From the Achievement & Performance screen you can create a shareable card showing your rank, rating and key stats, then send it through any app on your phone.

Is an account required?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your profile, settings, ratings and match history are stored on your device.

Can I play in my browser?

Yes — open the browser version and play instantly, no install. It covers solo play against bots and same-device play for all ten games, with achievement ranks and performance tracking saved in your browser. Wi-Fi multiplayer and voice chat need the Android app. Open the browser version.

Which platforms are supported?

Dual Play is available for Android on Google Play, and you can play the browser version on any modern desktop or mobile browser. There is no iPhone or iPad release at the moment.

Does Dual Play collect personal information?

Your game profile, settings, match history and rating stay on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for broadcast announcements; it does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Full details are in the Privacy Policy. Read the Privacy Policy.
