Tic Tac Toe flag artwork

Only country/region flags ship as images; every other emoji is drawn with the
device's own emoji font. These images are the fallback used when a device
can't draw a flag natively.

Source: the region-flags collection bundled with Noto Emoji
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, commit
99701e07d3d1fe1bdbcdb14964547f0fd592966f, third_party/region-flags/png).
The flags are in the public domain or otherwise exempt from copyright (see
LICENSE-region-flags.txt). Modified: resized so the longest side is 160 px
(proportions kept, no padding) and converted to WebP.

Upstream aliases (the collection itself maps these regions to the flag in
official use there; kept as-is, not substituted by this app):
  BV, SJ -> NO    CP, MF -> FR    DG -> IO    EA -> ES    HM -> AU    UM -> US

Files are named flag_<region>.webp (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, lower case; the
subdivision flags use their tag: flag_gbeng, flag_gbsct, flag_gbwls).
