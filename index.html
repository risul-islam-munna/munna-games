What ties them together

The same three rules, every time

Offline-first

The core of every game works with no connection — solo practice, pass-and-play, and local Wi-Fi. No connection required to have fun.

No accounts, ever

Open the app and play. No sign-up, no email, no feed to scroll. Your profile and results stay on your device.

Opponents worth beating

Real AI on every game, from gentle to genuinely hard — including perfect-play search where it fits. Fair by design, never rigged.
